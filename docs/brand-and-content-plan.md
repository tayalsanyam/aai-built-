# AAI Built — Brand and website plan

## Positioning
AAI Built (Augmented AI Built) combines Sanyam Tayal’s business experience with AI-assisted software development to build tools around the real workflows of SMEs.

Core line: Your next chapter. Built in.
Supporting promise: Extraordinary software. Built around your everyday.
Audience: SME owners outgrowing spreadsheets, disconnected tools, and inflexible software.
Visitor journey: Understand the possibility → explore a solution → see relevant work → understand the approach and founder → start a specific conversation.

## Visual direction
Apple-inspired clarity and product storytelling, with an original AAI identity. Bright neutral surfaces, restrained electric blue, expressive typography, rounded product surfaces, a real-time metallic 3D monogram, and focused scroll reveals. The blue core represents applied AI; connected layers represent business functions. Visitors can orbit the model, separate the layers, and pause motion. Respect reduced motion and provide an image fallback when WebGL is unavailable.

## Page map
- Home: the next chapter, interactive 3D brand, connected-business possibilities, projects, ownership, founder, approach.
- Solutions: interactive sales, operations, people, and applied AI explorer; practical growth mechanisms; industries; phased investment.
- Lead Engine: permitted business-data research and scraping, source recording, enrichment, deduplication, qualification, CRM assignment, and follow-up. Distributor example is explicitly illustrative.
- Projects: three product introductions, each linking to a detail page.
- Fusion Algo: trading strategy logic, workflow, and structured evaluation; no profit guarantees.
- Olready: the end-to-end operational CRM concept, customer context, handovers, and management visibility.
- Crew: attendance, call activity, consultant, and recruitment workflows.
- Our approach: discovery, mapping, iterative build, rollout; scope, hosting, source ownership, data access, and support agreed in writing.
- About: Sanyam’s 12 years and roles, HAG India and Myfresh, and the human expertise behind the brand.
- Contact: direct WhatsApp and email links; brief composer that opens a draft for the visitor to review and send.

## Content boundaries
Project facts are based on the founder’s supplied descriptions. Existing product URLs could not be read through the research tool. Workflow visuals are labelled concepts, not live product screenshots. Do not invent performance figures, client testimonials, feature inventories, pricing, delivery dates, or guaranteed business results. Describe new capabilities as potential custom scope. Data and software ownership, hosting, source access, support, and integrations are agreed per project.
